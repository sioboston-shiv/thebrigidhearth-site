An Fánai

The Wanderer · The Fool

beginnings · innocence · the leap · the open road · trust

Upright

You stand at the edge of something vast, and your only compass is the stirring in your chest. This is the moment before the journey — not foolish, but brave. Trust the not-knowing.

Reversed

You may be hesitating at the threshold, or perhaps leaping without looking one too many times. There is a difference between faith and recklessness, a stór. Feel for the ground beneath you before you step.

Irish lore

In Irish tradition, the wanderer walks between worlds — neither fully here nor in the Otherworld. The aisling poets wandered the roads after the old order fell, carrying nothing but their words and their trust in the road itself.
