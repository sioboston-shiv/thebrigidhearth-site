Pentacles

Seacht na gCloch

Seven of Stones · Seven of Pentacles

devotion · the edge · extremity · testing faith

Upright

Skellig Michael rises from the Atlantic like a prayer made of stone — the furthest point, the last edge, the place where devotion is tested by wind and wave. You are being asked how far you are willing to go for what you believe in. The edge is uncomfortable, but the view is extraordinary.

Reversed

Devotion has become obsession, or you have pushed yourself to an edge that is breaking you. Even the monks came down from the Skellig eventually. There is a difference between dedication and self-destruction. Pull back before you fall.

Irish lore

Skellig Michael (Sceilg Mhichíl) is a jagged rock eight miles off the Kerry coast, where monks built beehive huts and lived in extreme devotion from the 6th century. They climbed 600 stone steps above the Atlantic to pray. It is one of the most remote and awe-inspiring monastic sites in the world.
