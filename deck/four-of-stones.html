Pentacles

Ceathair na gCloch

Four of Stones · Four of Pentacles

ancestors · weight of the past · legacy · responsibility

Upright

The dolmen holds thousands of tonnes of stone in perfect balance, placed there by hands that are dust now. You are carrying the weight of those who came before — their hopes, their debts, their unfinished work. This is not a burden to resent; it is a trust to honour. Bear it with grace.

Reversed

The weight of the past is crushing you, or you are ignoring it entirely. The dolmen stands because of precise balance — too much weight on one side and it falls. Redistribute what you carry. Ask for help. Your ancestors did not build alone.

Irish lore

Dolmens are megalithic tombs found across Ireland — great capstones balanced on upright slabs, some weighing over 100 tonnes. In folk tradition, they were called "Diarmuid and Gráinne's Bed," after the lovers who fled across Ireland. They are monuments to the dead and to the living's capacity for monumental effort.
