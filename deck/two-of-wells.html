Cups

Dó na dTobar

Two of Wells · Two of Cups

small blessings · tenderness · partnership · gentle beginnings

Upright

Morning dew is the smallest water, the gentlest blessing — and sometimes the most precious. Pay attention to the quiet kindnesses, the small moments of connection. Not every gift arrives as a flood. Some come as dew on the grass at dawn.

Reversed

You may be overlooking the small blessings in search of something grander, or the tenderness being offered feels too small to matter. It is not. Morning dew waters the whole field, one blade of grass at a time.

Irish lore

In Irish folk tradition, dew collected on May morning — the first dew of Bealtaine — had healing properties. Women would wash their faces in it for beauty and health. It was the quietest, most everyday magic: the land's own gentleness, offered freely to those who rose early enough to receive it.
