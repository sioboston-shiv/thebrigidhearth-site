Swords

Spéir na nGaoth

Sky of Winds · King of Swords

freedom · the infinite · mastery of the air · transcendence · sovereignty of thought

Upright

The sky holds everything and clings to nothing. You have reached a place of mental clarity and freedom that most people only dream of. This is mastery — not of others, but of yourself, your thoughts, your perspective. The sky does not compete with the earth. It simply is.

Reversed

You may be so detached that you have floated away from what matters, or so high that the ground seems irrelevant. The sky without the earth is just emptiness. Bring your clarity back down. Freedom that serves no one is just loneliness with a better view.

Irish lore

The Irish sky was never empty in the old tradition — it was the realm of birds, of weather, of the gods of air and storm. The sky was where omens were read, where the aurora of the north painted messages, and where the souls of the worthy were believed to fly on their way to the Otherworld.
