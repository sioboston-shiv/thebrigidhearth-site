Wands

Deich na Géag

Ten of Branches · Ten of Wands

beauty · the Otherworld · completion · paradise

Upright

The apple branch is the key to the Otherworld — silver branch, crystal blossom, music that heals all sorrow. You have reached a place of beauty and completion in this cycle. Rest here. Eat the apple. Let the sweetness be enough.

Reversed

You may be chasing paradise instead of recognising it where you stand. Or perhaps the beauty before you feels unearned, and you cannot let yourself enjoy it. The Otherworld apple does not ask if you deserve it — it simply nourishes.

Irish lore

Apple (Quert) was the tree of the Otherworld. The silver branch with crystal apple blossoms was a passport to Emhain Abhlach, the paradise of apple trees. Manannán gave such a branch to Cormac mac Airt, and its music could ease any sorrow.
