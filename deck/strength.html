Major Arcana · VIII

Neart

Strength

inner strength · gentleness · courage · the wild tamed · endurance

Upright

The strongest thing you can do right now is be gentle — with yourself, with others, with the wild parts of your nature. True neart is not about overpowering; it is about meeting what is fierce with what is steady.

Reversed

You may be mistaking hardness for strength, or softness for weakness. If you are white-knuckling your way through, ease your grip. And if you have gone too soft, remember: gentleness is not the same as giving in.

Irish lore

In Irish tradition, strength was never solely physical. The concept of neart encompasses spiritual force, inner resolve, and the quiet power of endurance. Many Irish heroines — Macha, Gráinne, Medb — showed strength through will and cunning as much as through force.
