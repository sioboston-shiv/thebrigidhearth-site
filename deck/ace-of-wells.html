Aon na dTobar

Ace of Wells · Ace of Cups

beginnings · purity · emotional opening · blessing

Upright

A holy well bubbles up where the earth decides to offer its deepest gift. Something is opening in you — a feeling, a connection, a capacity for love you did not know you had. Cup your hands and drink. This water has been waiting for you.

Reversed

The well is there, but you may be afraid to drink — afraid of what feeling deeply will cost you. Or the water has been muddied by neglect. Clear the stones, clean the channel, and let the spring flow again.

Irish lore

Ireland has thousands of holy wells, many predating Christianity by millennia. They were places of healing, prayer, and petition, dressed with rags and coins. The water that rises from the earth was considered the purest form of blessing — the land offering itself to the people.
