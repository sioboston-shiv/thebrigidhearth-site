Major Arcana · VII

An Carbad

The Chariot

victory · skill · momentum · mastery · will

Upright

You have trained for this, and now the horses are running. Hold the reins with skill, not force — true victory comes from mastery, not brute strength. The road is open and you have earned the speed.

Reversed

Are you charging forward without direction, or has the chariot stalled because you are pulling the reins too tight? Momentum without purpose is just chaos. Find your aim before you whip the horses.

Irish lore

Lugh Lámhfhada — Lugh of the Long Arm — was the master of all skills, who won his place among the Tuatha Dé Danann by proving there was no single craft he could not perform. His chariot rode into battle at Moytura, where he led the gods to victory over the Fomorians.
