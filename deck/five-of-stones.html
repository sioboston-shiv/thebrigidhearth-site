Pentacles

Cúig na gCloch

Five of Stones · Five of Pentacles

sovereignty · the centre · authority · the seat of power

Upright

Tara is the centre, the seat of the high king, the navel of Ireland. You are being called to step into your sovereignty — not power over others, but the authority that comes from standing in your own centre. Claim your ground. You have the right to be here, and the right to lead.

Reversed

Power without legitimacy is just occupation. Are you claiming sovereignty you have not earned, or refusing a crown that is rightly yours? Tara chose its kings through prophecy and testing — the stone cried out for the true ruler. What would the stone say about you?

Irish lore

The Hill of Tara (Teamhair) was the seat of the High Kings of Ireland, where the Lia Fáil (Stone of Destiny) cried out beneath the feet of the rightful king. Tara was the symbolic centre of Ireland, the place where the five provinces met and sovereignty was conferred by the goddess of the land.
