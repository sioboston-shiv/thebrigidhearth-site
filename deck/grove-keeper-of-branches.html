Wands

Coimeádaí an Ghairdín

Grove-keeper of Branches · Knight of Wands

growth · tenacity · stewardship · patient nurturing

Upright

You are the one who tends the grove — not with grand gestures, but with daily care. Ivy climbs slowly and holds fast, and so must you. This is the work of stewardship: patient, unglamorous, and absolutely essential. The grove thrives because you show up.

Reversed

Are you tending the grove, or strangling it? Ivy can suffocate what it clings to if it grows unchecked. Your care may have become control, your nurturing a kind of possessiveness. Loosen your grip and let things grow on their own terms.

Irish lore

Ivy (Gort) was a symbol of tenacity and faithfulness in Irish tradition. It grows in spirals, clings to stone, and stays green through the harshest winter. Ivy-covered ruins are a signature of the Irish landscape — the green persistence of life over the grey of abandonment.
