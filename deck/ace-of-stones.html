Pentacles

Aon na gCloch

Ace of Stones · Ace of Pentacles

permanence · marking · foundation · solidity

Upright

Someone placed this stone here on purpose, long ago, and it has not moved since. You are being called to make your mark — to begin something with the weight and permanence of stone. This is not a fleeting impulse; it is a foundation. Build on it.

Reversed

You may be setting something in stone that should remain flexible, or you are afraid of permanence. Not everything needs to last forever, but some things should. Know the difference, and commit where commitment is due.

Irish lore

Ireland is a landscape defined by standing stones — ogham stones, boundary markers, pillar stones — each one placed with intention and left to endure. The act of raising a stone was a declaration: this happened here, this matters, this will be remembered.
