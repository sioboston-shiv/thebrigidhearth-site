Aon na Géag

Ace of Branches · Ace of Wands

new beginnings · purification · fresh start · potential

Upright

A new shoot is pushing through the soil, and it carries all the promise of the forest within it. This is the spark of something — a project, a path, a way of being. Tend it gently. Birch is the tree of beginnings, and beginnings ask for courage.

Reversed

The seed is there, but something is blocking the light. Perhaps you are overthinking the planting, or the soil needs clearing first. Do the small, necessary work before expecting growth.

Irish lore

Birch (Beith) is the first letter of the Ogham alphabet and the first tree to colonise bare ground after fire or ice. In Irish tradition, birch besoms were used to sweep away the old year and purify the threshold.
