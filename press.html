The Hearth is the first iOS app to treat Irish folk magic as a living daily practice. Built solo by Siobhan McAuley — who grew up in Dublin and made her home in Boston — it brings the four pillars of Irish tradition into a single premium daily companion: Brigid (an AI hearth-keeper grounded in Irish folk knowledge), the Celtic deck (78 bespoke cards in Irish and English), the Green Path (63 native plants), and the Wheel of the Year (eight fire festivals and the lunar calendar between them).

The story

Most apps in the "spiritual" category fall into one of two camps: cheap horoscope generators with ad walls, or generic wellness products with nothing rooted underneath. The Hearth is something different — a serious cultural product built with the craft of a premium consumer app, devoted to one specific living tradition.

Irish folk magic is not abstract. It has names, festivals, plants, cards, and a goddess who tends a perpetual flame at Kildare. The Hearth treats those particulars as the source material — not as theming, but as content. Brigid speaks in the old way, through stories, signs, and silence. The 78-card Celtic deck has Irish names (An Roth, An Fánai, An File) and original artwork drawn from native lore. The Green Path catalogues 63 plants the way our grandmothers used them. The Wheel marks Imbolc, Bealtaine, Lughnasadh, Samhain, and the four solar holidays between.

The launch on Bealtaine — the first day of the Celtic summer, the festival of fire and Brigid herself — is intentional.

The four pillars

Brigid at the Hearth

An AI companion built on Anthropic's Claude API, with a system prompt grounded in Irish folk tradition, the Wheel of the Year, the user's reading history, and the seasonal context. Brigid knows your decks, your cards, your festivals, and the rituals you've kept. She speaks in the old way — through stories, signs, and silence.

The Celtic Deck

Seventy-eight bespoke cards across four Irish suits — Branches, Wells, Winds, Stones — and twenty-two majors with Irish names (An Fánai, An File, An Roth). Original artwork, with toggleable Smith-Waite traditional naming for users coming from a Rider-Waite practice. Four spreads: Single, Three Card, Celtic Cross, and the Wheel of the Year.

The Green Path

Sixty-three native Irish plants, the conditions our ancestors used them for, and the traditional preparations. Cultural and educational material — explicitly not medical advice.

The Wheel of the Year

The eight fire festivals — Imbolc, Bealtaine, Lughnasadh, Samhain, and the four solar holidays between. Lunar phases. Threshold rituals. What to do when.
